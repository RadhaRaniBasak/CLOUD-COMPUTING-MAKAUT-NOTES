## What Unit 1 covers

Defining a cloud · NIST model · Cloud Cube model · deployment models (public, private, hybrid, community) · service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) with providers · cloud reference models (Buyya, CSA, NIST) · characteristics and benefits · composability, infrastructure, platforms, virtual appliances, communication protocols and applications · connecting clients to the cloud · IaaS (workloads, VPS partitioning, pods, aggregation, silos) · PaaS tools and development environments · SaaS, Open SaaS and SOA · Identity as a Service · Compliance as a Service.

The notes end with a quick revision sheet, a topic-wise PYQ bank (MCQs included, with answers) and practice questions. All 16 diagrams are in [`unit-1/images`](unit-1/images).

## How to use

- Study `notes.md` section by section; each topic lists the previous-year questions asked on it.
- Before the exam, read Section 16 (quick revision) and the PYQ answers.
- Write to the marks: 1 mark, one line; 5 marks, about one page; 15 marks, about three pages with a diagram.
- Practise drawing the diagrams; most long answers expect one.

## Exam pattern

70 marks in 3 hours: Group A, 10 MCQs × 1 mark; Group B, any 3 of 5 questions × 5 marks; Group C, any 3 of 5 questions × 15 marks. Check this against your latest paper.
